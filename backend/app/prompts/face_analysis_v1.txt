## Scoring Dimensions (0-100 each)

### hair_health (0-100)
Observe: shine, volume, texture, frizz, scalp visibility, hair density at temples/part.
Higher = healthier-looking hair. Consider lighting conditions.

### skin_quality (0-100)
Observe: evenness of tone, hydration appearance, blemishes, under-eye circles, pore visibility, overall radiance.
Higher = better skin appearance. Estimate a "skin_age" relative to typical appearance.

### stress_level (0-100)
Observe: tension in forehead/jaw, furrowed brows, under-eye darkness, skin dullness, facial muscle tightness.
Higher = MORE stressed appearance. Look for relaxation vs tension cues.

### sleep_quality (0-100)
Observe: puffiness around eyes, dark circles, droopy eyelids, overall alertness of expression, skin pallor.
Higher = BETTER rested appearance. Consider eye openness and skin freshness.

For each dimension provide:
- score (integer 0-100)
- observations (2-3 specific things you noticed)
- tips (1-2 actionable suggestions)
- For skin_quality, also include estimated_skin_age (integer)

Also provide:
- overall_summary: 1-2 encouraging sentences about their wellness appearance today
- top_recommendation: single most impactful tip based on lowest-scoring area
