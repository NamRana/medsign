AI health companion — understand symptoms before the doctor

### What does your company do?
MedSign is an AI-powered health assessment app that helps people understand their symptoms, analyze medical images, interpret lab reports, and track health trends over time. We sit in the gap between "I feel sick" and "I need to see a doctor" — where 80% of people currently turn to Google and get panic or misinformation.

### Why did you pick this idea to work on?
Healthcare in India (and globally) has a massive gap: there's nothing between free-but-unreliable (Google, Reddit) and paid-but-inaccessible (doctor visits, teleconsults). 1mg sells medicines. MediBuddy connects you to doctors. Nobody helps you figure out if you even need a doctor in the first place.

I've seen family members panic over normal lab values and ignore serious symptoms — because they had no way to understand what their body was telling them. MedSign fixes that.

### What's new about what you're making?
Three things existing health apps don't do:

1. **Multimodal AI assessment** — Users can describe symptoms via text/voice AND upload photos of visible conditions. We use Google's MedGemma and Gemini to analyze both together, not just match keywords to a disease database.

2. **Lab report intelligence** — Upload a PDF lab report, get instant plain-English explanations. "Your HbA1c is 6.8% — this means pre-diabetic range. Here's what to discuss with your doctor." No other consumer app does this.

3. **Health timeline** — Every interaction (symptom chat, image analysis, lab report, face scan) feeds into a unified health timeline with trends, risk scores, and body system mapping. Your health isn't a series of isolated events — MedSign connects the dots.

### How far along are you?
Live product deployed (web + PWA). Full-stack built: FastAPI backend, Next.js frontend, Supabase database, Google Gemini AI integration. Features working: conversational symptom assessment, medical image analysis, lab report parsing, face scan health tracking, family health management, admin review dashboard.

### What is your revenue model?
Freemium SaaS:
- **Free tier:** 5 symptom assessments/month, basic lab report analysis
- **Premium ($5-10/month):** Unlimited assessments, image analysis, family accounts, health trends, priority AI processing
- **Future:** B2B API for clinics/pharmacies wanting to add AI triage, insurance company partnerships for preventive health data

### Who are your competitors?
- **Ada Health / Babylon Health** — symptom checkers, but keyword/decision-tree based, not conversational AI. No image analysis or lab reports.
- **WebMD Symptom Checker** — checkbox form, generic results, ad-driven. No personalization.
- **1mg / PharmEasy** — pharmacies, not health assessment. They're where you go AFTER knowing what's wrong.
- **MediBuddy / Practo** — teleconsult platforms. They connect you to doctors, they don't help you understand your health independently.

MedSign is the only app combining conversational AI + image analysis + lab reports + health tracking in one consumer product.

### How do users find you?
1. **SEO/Content:** "What does my lab report mean" / "is this rash serious" — high-intent health queries with no good answers
2. **WhatsApp/Social sharing:** "Upload your lab report, get instant explanation" is inherently shareable
3. **Pharmacy/clinic partnerships:** MedSign as a white-label triage tool before teleconsult
4. **Word of mouth:** Health is personal — people tell family first

### What do you understand that others don't?
The biggest healthcare problem isn't access to doctors — it's the anxiety and confusion BEFORE the doctor. People don't need more ways to book appointments. They need to understand what their body is telling them so they can make informed decisions. The 10-minute doctor visit doesn't fix health literacy — an AI companion that's available 24/7 and remembers your entire health history does.

### How will you make money?
Consumer subscription ($5-10/month premium) is the starting point. But the real business is B2B:
- Pharmacies (1mg, PharmEasy) pay for AI triage to reduce unnecessary doctor referrals
- Insurance companies pay for preventive health data to reduce claims
- Employers pay for employee health screening tools
- Clinics pay for pre-visit patient intake AI
